# V1.1 Manual QA Plan

Run after applying migrations to a disposable test database and building the app for a representative device. Do not use production business data.

## Categories and registration

1. Register a new business and verify the six starter categories appear exactly once.
2. Add a category, rename it, and verify the renamed category remains selectable in product create/edit and filters.
3. Verify duplicate names are rejected and a category with assigned products cannot be deleted.
4. Verify users from another business cannot read or mutate these categories.

## Theme

1. Select System, Light and Dark in settings and verify the full app follows each selection.
2. Force close and reopen after selecting Light and Dark; verify the selection persists.
3. Change device theme while System is selected and verify the app follows it.

## Legal consent

1. Open each legal document from registration and settings; confirm template label, version and update date are visible.
2. Verify registration cannot continue without acceptance.
3. Complete signup and confirm the API stores version 1.0 and an acceptance timestamp for the owner.
4. Confirm no legal-compliance claim is shown in the app.

## Existing V1 regression checks

1. Sign in as owner, manager, cashier and storekeeper; confirm expected role access.
2. Search products by name/SKU and scan a barcode; filter by category and stock status.
3. Complete cash, M-Pesa demo and credit-sale flows; inspect receipt, stock movement and customer balance.
4. Receive stock, adjust stock, count stock and verify low/out-of-stock alerts and unread badge behavior.
5. Test offline banner, preserved cart, reconnection and retry without duplicate sales.
6. Open reports, customer balances, sales history and receipt sharing.

## Push notifications (after Firebase setup)

1. Grant and deny permission; verify settings reflect the chosen state.
2. Exercise foreground, background and terminated delivery for each enabled event.
3. Verify deduplication, unread state, deep-link destination, invalid-token cleanup and preference opt-out.
4. Verify marketing stays disabled unless explicitly enabled.
5. Create a credit sale, record a customer payment, cross the configured debt threshold, add/change staff, perform a first staff login, and verify daily summary and optional new-sale delivery obey per-user preferences.

## Release gates

- Apply migrations and run backend integration checks against a disposable DB.
- Verify tenant isolation, role restrictions, registration consent and rollback behavior.
- Build/install signed release artifacts; never distribute a debug-signed release.
- Measure cold start, dashboard, product list, checkout, reports, frame timing, API latency and package size on the same device for before/after comparison.
