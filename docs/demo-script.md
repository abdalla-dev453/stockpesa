# StockPesa five-minute demo

**Audience:** a small-shop owner or pilot partner. Use the local seeded demo business only. Keep the development API and demo credentials off the public internet.

## 0:00–0:30 — The problem

“Small shops can lose sales when shelves run empty, and lose money when stock and credit sales live in notebooks. StockPesa puts stock movement, checkout, receipts, and customer debt in one place.”

## 0:30–1:00 — Dashboard

Sign in as `owner@stockpesa.app`. Point out today’s sales, product count, low/out-of-stock counts, and alerts. Explain that the summary comes from the business API and refreshes on pull-down.

## 1:00–1:40 — Add stock

Open Products and add a familiar local item such as maize flour, cooking oil, or soap with a price and minimum-stock level. Open Stock and receive a small quantity. Show the on-hand number and movement record.

## 1:40–2:30 — Scan and sell

Open Sell, scan a product barcode (or search by name/SKU), add quantity, and take cash. Show the success state and receipt. Return to product detail and show the automatic stock reduction and sale movement.

## 2:30–3:00 — M-Pesa manual record and mock

Record an already-received M-Pesa payment with a receipt number and amount. Show the reference and settled amount in the payment/receipt detail. If using the local mock instead, show the pending request and callback confirmation. Be explicit that manual recording does not initiate STK Push and mock mode is not a live Safaricom integration.

## 3:00–3:40 — Credit and customer payment

Make a credit sale for the seeded customer, “Mama Otis.” Open Customers, show the outstanding balance and unpaid sale, then record a partial payment. Show the reduced customer and sale balances.

## 3:40–4:15 — Low stock and reports

Open Alerts and filter low/out-of-stock. Show the item and read state. Open Reports to show sales, gross profit, payment methods, stock values, product performance, and customer debt; export the CSV as owner.

## 4:15–5:00 — Roles and next step

Open Staff as owner and show role management. Briefly sign in as cashier to demonstrate that detailed reports, staff administration, and returns are restricted. Close with the pilot proposal: onboard a small group of shops, measure checkout time, stock variance, stockouts, weekly active usage, and paid retention before investing in live M-Pesa and broader rollout.
