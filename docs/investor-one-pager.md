# StockPesa | Investor one-pager

## Problem

Independent retailers often track purchases, stock, sales, customer credit, and receipts in separate notebooks or spreadsheets. That makes it hard to see what is available, what is selling, and who owes the business.

## Solution

StockPesa is an Android-first stock and sales tool that ties inventory movements to checkout and customer balances. A merchant can search or scan products, record cash/M-Pesa-mock/bank/card/credit sales, issue a receipt, manage returns, and review stock and sales reports.

## Target market

Initial learning market: owner-operated neighborhood shops and small retailers in Kenya that need a simple daily stock and sales workflow. The V1 should be validated with a focused pilot before estimating a total addressable market or geographic expansion.

## Business model hypothesis

- Free or assisted onboarding for a short pilot.
- Monthly subscription per shop, with optional paid onboarding/support.
- Later validation: multi-branch, live payment-provider integrations, and accounting/export integrations as paid tiers.

Pricing and willingness to pay are hypotheses; the pilot must establish them.

## Competitive advantage hypothesis

- One transaction trail connecting stock movement, sale, payment, receipt, customer debt, and return.
- Designed for Kenyan retail workflows, KES, common mobile payment methods, barcode entry, and credit sales.
- A focused, low-friction Android workflow rather than a broad enterprise suite.

These are positioning hypotheses, not independently verified market claims.

## V1 features

Product/category catalogue and CSV import; stock intake, adjustment, and counts; barcode scanning; cash, credit, manual M-Pesa receipt recording and M-Pesa mock sales; customer payments; returns; PDF receipts; alerts; dashboards and reports; staff roles; tenant-separated API data.

## Metrics to validate

- Time from opening checkout to completed sale (target under 30 seconds for a familiar item).
- Stock count variance before and after adoption.
- Stockout frequency for tracked products.
- Weekly active shops and sales recorded per active shop.
- Receipt sharing and credit-payment usage.
- 30/60/90-day shop retention and conversion to paid plan.
- Support burden and onboarding completion time.

## Roadmap

**Pilot readiness:** security review, signed Android release, data backups, operational monitoring, and in-shop workflow observation.

**V1.1:** live M-Pesa provider integration after sandbox/settlement testing, operational dashboards, resilient sync for clearly bounded workflows, and merchant-requested usability fixes.

**Later:** multi-branch reporting, accounting integrations, richer exports, and additional localization, prioritized from pilot evidence.
