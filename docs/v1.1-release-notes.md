# StockPesa V1.1 — Quality & Trust Release Notes

## Included in this implementation slice

- New business registration creates six editable starter categories.
- Category management supports renaming while retaining existing create/delete safeguards and product counts.
- Users can choose System, Light or Dark appearance in Business settings; the choice persists on the device.
- Per-user notification preferences are stored by the API; marketing defaults off. Authenticated token lifecycle, stock-transition outbox, and FCM HTTP v1 delivery are implemented.
- A business-only Market Insights view shows 7/30-day sales, best/slow movers, stock health and estimated run-out timing, labeled as estimates.
- Updated StockPesa launcher identity with 48–1024px PNG exports, Android adaptive/monochrome resources, iOS icon assets, wordmark, onboarding and empty-state art.
- Registration links to privacy and terms templates, requires acceptance, and records the accepted template version and timestamp.
- Privacy and terms drafts are available from settings and registration and are clearly marked as templates requiring Kenyan legal review.

## Not yet release-enabled

- Live push delivery requires Firebase Android/iOS app IDs, Android/iOS platform setup, APNs configuration, and a server service-account secret. Failed-login alerts and delivery-status inspection UI remain follow-up work.
- Market insight extensions and performance measurements require a representative runtime environment and verified business data.
- Adaptive/monochrome launcher assets and final visual-system pass remain outstanding.
- Release signing and security verification remain release gates documented in `qa-audit-report.md`.
