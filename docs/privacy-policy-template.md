## Information processed

StockPesa may process business name and type; owner and staff names, phone numbers and email addresses; optional business location; product, category, stock, supplier, sales, payment and audit records; customer contact and credit information entered by a merchant; device and app diagnostics; and notification device tokens.

## Purposes and lawful basis

Information is used to provide inventory, checkout, customer-account and reporting services; authenticate users and enforce roles; protect accounts, detect misuse and support auditability; deliver requested service notifications; maintain and improve the service; and respond to support requests. The operator must document a lawful basis for each purpose and explain any required consent or legitimate-interest assessment.

## Sharing and transfers

StockPesa does not sell personal information. The operator may share necessary information with hosting, database, notification, analytics and support providers that operate the service, and where disclosure is required by law or reasonably needed to protect users and the service. Before launch, list relevant processors, processing locations, safeguards and cross-border transfer arrangements.

## Retention and rights

Business data is retained while an account is active and as needed for security, disputes and legal obligations. The operator must publish specific deletion and backup-retention periods. Users may contact the operator to request access, correction or deletion, object to or restrict processing, or withdraw consent where consent is the basis. Users may also complain to Kenya’s Office of the Data Protection Commissioner. Requests should go to the contact above. Some records may need to be retained where law requires.

## Security and children

Production service traffic should use HTTPS and access should be restricted by account and business role. No system can guarantee absolute security; the operator should maintain incident-response procedures and meet applicable notification duties. StockPesa is intended for business users and is not designed for children.

## Changes

Material changes will be reflected in the policy version and effective date. The current version is available in the app and through the operator’s published support channel.
